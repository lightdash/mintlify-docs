---
title: "Integrations"
description: "Connect Lightdash to Slack, Google Sheets, SQL clients over the Postgres wire protocol, and Snowflake Cortex."
---

Lightdash works with the tools your team already uses. Send chart results to Slack and Google Sheets, deliver alerts and scheduled reports, query your semantic layer from any SQL client, and operate Lightdash agentically from Snowflake Cortex.

Two closely related integrations are documented in their own product areas: connect a [dbt project](/semantic-layer/dbt/projects) to build your semantic layer, and add the [Lightdash MCP server](/agents/lightdash-mcp) so AI assistants can explore your data and run queries directly.
