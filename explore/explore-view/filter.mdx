---
title: "Filtering in the Explore view"
description: "Add filters to a query from the Filters tab, the field sidebar, or the results table, and group them into nested conditions."
---

Filters restrict the data a query returns. Filtering a chart in the Explore view changes that chart alone — to filter every tile on a dashboard at once, see [dashboard filters](/explore/dashboards/filter). For the filter types and operators available on each field type, see the [filters reference](/explore/filters-reference).

There are three places to add a filter, and they all produce the same thing.

## From the Filters tab

Open the `Filters` toggle, then click `Add filter`.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/explore-view/filter/adding-filters-from-toggle-1-ce5fc6c2ad6259dcae43a9ff16937ffc.jpg" alt="Adding a filter from the Filters tab in the Explore view" />
</Frame>

Select the field to filter from the drop-down list, then choose the filtering options.

## From the sidebar

Click the `options` for a field in the sidebar list, then click `add filter`. The filter icon that appears when you hover over a dimension or metric does the same thing.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/explore-view/filter/add-filter-sidebar-32d08090746e3cd362b982a7238b9b6f.jpg" alt="Adding a filter from a field in the Explore sidebar" />
</Frame>

## From the results table

Once you have results, click the three-dot menu of the column you want to filter on and choose `Filter by [field name]`, or click a value in the table to filter by that value.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/explore-view/filter/add-filter-results-table-52825577533d8ee37e5b9031e7bd54d0.jpg" alt="Adding a filter from the results table column menu" />
</Frame>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/explore-view/filter/add-filter.png" alt="The filter added from the results table showing in the Filters section" />
</Frame>

## Nested filters

To nest filters, click the options button on the right-hand side of a filter and choose `convert to group`.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/explore-view/filter/convert-to-filter-group-c9f2d2a4a0260d5f8b869c9d156b07a9.png" alt="Converting a filter into a filter group" />
</Frame>

Then add a filter to the group by clicking `add filter`.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/explore-view/filter/nested-filters-eae25b7df33e4f47b1408d8a7bfd8ce5.png" alt="A nested filter group with two conditions" />
</Frame>
