At the scheduled time, Lightdash will run the query and check the results. If the value in the first row of the chart's results, in the chart's sort order, meets the alerting condition, the alert will be sent. Other rows aren't checked. To alert when any row meets the condition, sort the chart by the alert field, descending for **is greater than** or ascending for **is less than**, so the first row holds the most extreme value. This notification will continue to be sent if the condition is true until it is manually disabled or deleted.

You can create or select an existing saved chart, add an alert to it, and tell Lightdash when you’d like the send out your update. You can set up as many alerts to a saved chart as you like, and if you make any changes to the content, Lightdash will update the alert the next time they’re sent. Alerts can be sent to an email address or into Slack (assuming your org has [added Slack](/integrations/slack)).

For security reasons, delivered files expire after 3 days. Images in older emails and Slack shares break once they expire and there's no way to retrieve them, so download anything you want to keep from the alert itself.

<Tip>
  **Self-hosted: fix broken images in emails behind firewalls**

  If your Lightdash instance is behind a firewall, email clients like Gmail may not be able to load images from your internal URLs. Set `EMAIL_SMTP_IMAGE_INLINE_CID=true` to embed images directly into emails as attachments. See [SMTP configuration](/self-host/customize-deployment/environment-variables#smtp) for details.
</Tip>

## Enabling alerts

Email alerts are available on all Lightdash Cloud plans.

To set up Slack, an organization admin will have to [add the Slack integration](/integrations/slack).

## Creating an alert

Only users with [admin, developer or edit access](/workspace-admin/roles) can create alerts. To create an alert, click on the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of your chart, then click on `Alerts`.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/create-alerts/alert-three-dot-menu.png" alt="The three-dot menu open in the top-right corner of a saved chart, with the Alerts option in the list"/>
</Frame>

You'll get a modal that pops up and shows you the list of existing alerts for your saved chart, and gives you the option to `Create new`.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/create-alerts/alert-create-new-modal.png" alt="The alerts modal for a saved chart, listing existing alerts with a Create new button"/>
</Frame>

Once you click on `Create new`, you'll be brought to a configuration screen for your new alert.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/create-alerts/configure-alert.png" alt="The alert configuration screen, with fields for name, condition, run frequency and where to send the alert"/>
</Frame>

Here, you can configure your alert. The options are:

* **Alert name**: this is the name of your alert.
* **Conditions**: Configure the condition that is going to be evaluated against the first result(s).
   * `Alert field` The numeric field is going to be evaluated
   * `Condition`: Operator we are going to use to evaluate your value.
         * **is greater/less than**: Check if the `first` value in your results is greater/less than your threshold value.
         * **increased/decreased by**: Verify whether the percentage change in the first value relative to the second value exceeds the specified threshold, either by increasing or decreasing.
   * **Notify me only once**: send the alert the first time the condition is met, then pause it. The alert shows as paused in your list of alerts until you turn its toggle back on. Leave this unticked to be notified every time the condition is met.
* **Run frequency**: This is the frequency at which Lightdash runs a query to check your data for changes. (You will be notified if the conditions on the latest value are met) . All times for the scheduler are in UTC.
* **Send to**: here, you can pick if you want to send your alert to Slack, email, or both. You can add as many Slack channels and email addresses as you'd like to here. You can send an alert to a person in Slack using `@persons-name` or to a channel using `#channel-name`. The Lightdash app needs to already be a member of any Slack channel you add — see [Saved chart alerts in Slack](/integrations/slack#saved-chart-alerts-in-slack) for how to invite it.

Alerts don't have a custom message. Email and Slack alerts name the chart and list the condition that was met. The field in the condition is named from its field ID rather than its label in the chart, so `orders_total_revenue` appears as **Orders total revenue**. Each alert also includes an image of the chart.

## Editing and deleting your alerts

To edit an alert, click on the pencil-icon next to the alert you want to edit. This will bring you back to the configuration screen for your alert, where you can make any changes you'd like. In case you want to delete a alert, click on the trash-icon next to the alert you want to delete.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/explore/create-alerts/edit-delete-alert.png" alt="The alerts list, each row with a pencil icon to edit, a trash icon to delete, and a toggle to pause it"/>
</Frame>

You can also pause alerts by clicking the toggle button on the list of alerts.

Lightdash also pauses an alert when an error means it can't run until someone fixes it, for example when the alert field is no longer in the chart's results, the warehouse credentials are wrong or the warehouse can't be reached, the alert has no recipients, or it sends to Slack and your organization's Slack integration was removed. Fix the cause, then turn the alert's toggle back on.
