# Cluster: data apps

## Summary

`main` grew the data apps area by three pages and rewrote two facts the branch still states the old way. The three new pages are all real content with no branch home: a local/CLI development workflow (split out of `data-apps.mdx`), an app-specific scheduled-deliveries and Google Sheets page, and a maps/globes walkthrough. The two rewrites are genuine conflicts: (a) **app identity moved from `appUuid` to `slug`**, which invalidates the entire `Data apps as code` section on `data-apps.mdx` and every app flag in `workflow/cli/reference.mdx`; (b) **"data app visualizations" was renamed to "project chart types" and moved out of data apps entirely** — `main` deleted `guides/data-apps/visualizations.mdx` and folded a rewritten version into `references/chart-types/custom-charts.mdx`, while the branch kept it as `data-apps/create-visualizations.mdx`. The branch's independent move of `custom-charts.mdx` to `explore/chart-types/` is compatible with `main`'s decision but does not satisfy it — the content consolidation still has to be ported, and `data-apps/create-visualizations.mdx` should be deleted with a redirect.

Two commits landing in this area were not in the brief's list: `03a8b38` (theme-as-code CLI workflow, adds ~79 lines to `themes.mdx`) and `9b83db4` (embed snippet on `embedding/data-apps.mdx`). Both are mapped below.

## Flags

1. **`Building data apps locally` is not a Guide** (row 1). Applying the litmus honestly: it makes essentially one debatable recommendation ("treat git as the source of truth"); the advice does not transfer off the Lightdash CLI; and stripping the steps *does* leave substance (slug identity, versioning semantics, the server build model, the dependency security model) — but that substance is feature documentation, not opinion. It's plain docs. `main`'s gerund title reads guide-ish, which is why I'd rename it `Data apps as code`.

2. **Slug vs UUID is a live contradiction, not a stylistic one.** The branch states `appUuid` is "the source of truth for identity" (`data-apps.mdx:316`) and documents `--apps <appUuids...>` in `workflow/cli/reference.mdx`. `main` states the slug is identity and `appUuid`/`projectUuid` are legacy compatibility fields. If rows 1–3 land partially, the corpus ships both claims. Land them together.

3. **"Data app visualizations" → "project chart types" is a feature rename, not just a page move** (rows 9–12). Every surface using the old name has to change in one pass: `data-apps.mdx` §Visualizations, `explore/chart-types.mdx`, `explore/chart-types/custom-charts.mdx`, `workflow/cli/reference.mdx` (two flag descriptions referencing `vizSchema`), `docs.json` nav + redirects. Note the CLI manifest field is still literally `vizSchema` — that's a product string, keep it, but describe it as "the chart type's declared inputs" as `main` does.

4. **Same fact in two places after row 13.** `data-apps.mdx` §`Refreshing the preview` and `semantic-layer/caching.mdx` both describe per-app cache invalidation on refresh. Caching is the canonical home; consider trimming the `data-apps.mdx` paragraph to the behavioral statement plus the link rather than restating the mechanism.

5. **`data-apps.mdx` is an area root page carrying 5,321 words of feature docs.** Principle 12 says the landing is "a short orientation plus the auto-rendered child directory"; principle 10 triggers a size review past ~3,000. Row 2 takes it to ~4,084 and rows 5/11 barely move it. **`decide`**: leave it (accept a fat root page, ship the catch-up, review separately), or use this catch-up as the moment to lift `Creating a new app` / `Sharing an app` / `Promoting an app` into `data-apps/` children and leave a real landing page. My recommendation is to ship the catch-up as mapped and file the split as its own cleanup — folding a root-page restructure into a 19-row catch-up makes the diff unreviewable.

6. **`## Best practices` heading on `data-apps.mdx:365`.** The IA rule bans the phrase from titles and slugs; a top-level section heading is close enough to earn a rename. Cheap fix while row 2 is open — something like `## Getting better results`.

7. **`data-apps/customize-themes.mdx` frontmatter mismatch** (pre-existing on the branch, not caused by `main`): slug is verb-first `customize-themes` and it declares `doc-type: tutorial`, but the title is the bare noun `Themes`. Row 15 adds a large as-code reference section to it, which pushes it further from a Tutorial. Worth a look while it's open.

8. **Two commits landed in this cluster that weren't in the brief**: `03a8b38` (row 15) and `9b83db4` (row 16). Row 16 in particular is stranded — it depends on a snippet the embed cluster has to create.
