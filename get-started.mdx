---
title: "Getting started"
description: "Set up your first Lightdash project and learn the fundamentals."
---

Start with the quickstart to get your dbt project Lightdash-ready, connect your database, and invite your team. From there, pick the track that matches what you're doing next.

If you're consuming data, the exploring track walks through querying tables, building charts and dashboards, and sharing insights. If you're building the semantic layer, the developer track covers adding tables, and creating metrics and dimensions.
