---
title: "Personal settings"
description: "Manage your own tokens, warehouse connections, and display preferences in Lightdash"
---

These are settings scoped to you rather than your project or organization: personal access tokens for authenticating with the CLI and API, and personal warehouse connections for when you want to query with your own credentials instead of the project's shared connection.

You can also set your timezone display preference here, which controls how dates and times are shown to you across Lightdash.
