This guide is for the data team planning a training or onboarding session for business users — people who explore dashboards and charts, rather than build the semantic layer behind them.

## Build the session around your own dashboards

Base the session on hands-on tasks that use the dashboards and metrics your business users already have, not a generic sample dataset. Sequence the tasks from simple to advanced, matching how technical the audience is, so everyone has something to work on.

Run the tasks in small groups that work through them together, rather than one person demoing Lightdash while everyone else watches. People learn more by exploring Lightdash themselves than by watching someone else click around.

## Let people practice on their own first

Before or alongside a live session, point attendees to [Learn](/explore/learn): each person gets a guided walkthrough that teaches a Lightdash feature on a sample project, without touching your team's real dashboards or data. An organization admin needs to enable Learn before anyone can use it; once they have, it's open to everyone in the organization.
