Lightdash builds your BI layer from a semantic layer you define as code, either in a dbt project or in [Lightdash YAML](/semantic-layer/yaml) files that point at tables already in your warehouse. Every setup follows the same three steps. The details depend on how you model your data, where Lightdash runs, and how it reaches your warehouse.

1. **Define your semantic layer.** Install the Lightdash CLI and generate your first Tables so there's something to explore. Start from [your dbt project](/integrations/dbt#set-up-a-dbt-project) or [Lightdash YAML](/semantic-layer/yaml).
2. **[Connect your warehouse and project](/integrations/connect-project).** Add warehouse credentials and choose how Lightdash gets your project: deploy from the CLI or connect your git repository so Lightdash stays in sync. Warehouse-specific connection details for BigQuery, Snowflake, Postgres, and more live in this step. If your warehouse sits in a private network, you also need to [connect through an SSH tunnel](/integrations/connect-through-ssh-tunnel).
3. **[Invite your team](/workspace-admin/invite-new-users).** Bring in teammates with the right roles, or let people with your work email domain join automatically.

When your project is live, pick the track that matches what you're doing next: [explore your data](/get-started/explore-your-data) to query, visualize, and share, or [build your semantic layer](/get-started/build-your-semantic-layer) to define metrics and dimensions as code.

## Find your setup path

Answer a few questions to get the version of these steps that fits your setup, each linked to the page with the details.

<Accordion title="Setup paths as text">
The picker asks up to seven questions. Which questions appear depends on your earlier answers, and each answer adds steps to your path. The finished path groups steps in this order: deploy, warehouse, project, team.

1. **Where does Lightdash run?**
   - **Lightdash Cloud:** go to question 3.
   - **Self-hosted:** deploy Lightdash with the [production deployment checklist](/self-host/production-deployment-checklist). Use Tier 1 for an evaluation and Tier 2 before production. Then answer question 2.
2. **Which edition are you deploying?** Self-hosted only.
   - **Open source:** no extra step.
   - **Enterprise:** before you deploy, [request a license key](/self-host/enterprise-features#get-a-license-key) from the Lightdash team and apply it to every container.
3. **How is your data modeled?**
   - **dbt project:** [set up your dbt project](/integrations/dbt#set-up-a-dbt-project). Install the CLI, log in, and generate Tables for the models you want in Lightdash.
   - **Lightdash YAML:** [define your semantic layer in Lightdash YAML](/semantic-layer/yaml) by adding `lightdash.config.yml` and your model files.
   - **Not modeled yet (Beta):** connect your warehouse and [start with Aurora](/get-started/agentic-onboarding) to query it with an AI agent, then add a semantic layer later. Skip question 6.
   - **Just looking:** explore [the demo project](https://demo.lightdash.com) and [invite whoever owns warehouse access](/workspace-admin/invite-new-users) so they can pick up setup later. Your path ends here.
4. **Which warehouse?** BigQuery, Snowflake, Databricks, Redshift, Postgres, Trino, ClickHouse, Athena, or DuckDB. Create a read-only service user or role for Lightdash and pick an authentication method from your warehouse's [connection settings](/integrations/connect-project#1-connect-to-a-warehouse).
5. **Can Lightdash reach your warehouse?**
   - **Yes, over the internet:** on Lightdash Cloud, [allow-list Lightdash's static IP addresses](/integrations/connect-project#adding-lightdashs-static-ip-addresses-to-your-allow-list) if your warehouse or firewall restricts inbound traffic.
   - **No, it's in a private network:** for Postgres or Redshift, [set up an SSH tunnel](/integrations/connect-through-ssh-tunnel) through a bastion host. SSH tunnels support only Postgres and Redshift, so for other warehouses [contact support](/support) about your network setup.
6. **How will Lightdash get your project?** dbt and Lightdash YAML only. A git connection is recommended for production. See [compare sync methods](/integrations/dbt/projects#which-method-should-i-use).
   - **CLI first, then connect git (recommended):** create your project from the CLI ([dbt](/integrations/dbt#create-your-project) or [Lightdash YAML](/semantic-layer/yaml)). For dbt, [replace the credentials](/integrations/connect-project#1-connect-to-a-warehouse) copied from your local profile with a shared service account. Lightdash YAML projects deploy without credentials, so add them in project settings. Then answer question 7.
   - **Connect git from the start (recommended):** [create your project in the app](/integrations/connect-project#open-up-your-lightdash-instance-to-get-started) from **Organization settings** → **All projects** → **Create new**. [Connect your warehouse](/integrations/connect-project#1-connect-to-a-warehouse) with a service account. Then answer question 7.
   - **CLI only:** for a quick proof of concept or when you have no repository access. Create your project from the CLI and update its warehouse credentials as in the first option. CLI projects don't refresh from the UI, so [deploy from CI](/workflow/set-up-ci-cd#deploy-changes-to-lightdash) on every merge.
   - **Connect dbt Cloud:** dbt only. Create the project in the app and choose [dbt Cloud](/integrations/connect-project#dbt-cloud) as the connection type. Connecting through your git repository is recommended unless you need dbt Cloud-only features such as cross-project references.
7. **Which git host?** GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, or Azure DevOps. Lightdash YAML projects support GitHub and Bitbucket. [Connect your repository](/integrations/connect-project#2-import-a-dbt-project), branch, and project directory ([Lightdash YAML](/semantic-layer/yaml#connect-through-github-or-bitbucket-cloud)), then [set up CI/CD](/workflow/set-up-ci-cd) to preview and validate pull requests and refresh Lightdash after each merge.

Every finished path except **Just looking** ends with two steps: [invite your team](/workspace-admin/invite-new-users) with allowed email domains and default roles, and [build your first chart](/get-started/explore-your-data).
