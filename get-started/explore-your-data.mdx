This track is for people who consume data in Lightdash. It walks the explorer journey end to end: find your way around the content your team has already built, learn the metrics and dimensions that power every query, run your own explorations, and turn the results into charts and dashboards you can share.

Work through the steps below in order. You'll start by [exploring your content](/get-started/explore-your-data/explore-your-content) and getting to know [metrics and dimensions](/get-started/explore-your-data/intro-metrics-dimensions), then [query from Tables](/get-started/explore-your-data/use-explores), [share what you find](/get-started/explore-your-data/share-insights), and finally [build](/get-started/explore-your-data/build-a-dashboard) and [use dashboards](/get-started/explore-your-data/interact-with-dashboards).
