Agentic onboarding is the flow every new organization goes through. You sign up, set up your organization, connect a warehouse or a live data source, and land on a homepage where you can ask an AI agent about your data straight away. The project needs no dbt models, semantic layer, or explores to start: the agent can answer from your warehouse tables with SQL.

If you already have a dbt project you want to connect, you can still follow the [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    On the **Create an account** page, sign up with single sign-on if your instance offers it, or with your work email.

    When the instance can send email, the form asks only for your email address and Lightdash sends a 6-digit code to verify it. You set no password. Self-hosted instances without SMTP ask for a name and password instead; see [email-only signup](/self-host/customize-deployment/use-sso-login-for-self-hosted-lightdash#email-only-signup-and-otp-login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your organization">
    The setup page has two parts:

    - **Name your organization.** The name is prefilled from a company email domain. For company domains you can also let anyone with an email at that domain join.
    - **Tell us about you.** Enter your first name, last name, and role.

    People who join an existing organization from an invite see only the second part.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a source">
    The **Connect your data** page lists the warehouses you can connect: BigQuery, Snowflake, Databricks, Postgres, Redshift, ClickHouse, Athena, Trino, and DuckDB. You can also:

    - **Try the demo project** to explore sample data first, when your instance offers it.
    - **Start with a live data source** such as PostHog, instead of a warehouse. See [Start without a warehouse](#start-without-a-warehouse).
    - **Invite a teammate to connect it** if you don't have the warehouse details. See [Hand the warehouse connection to a teammate](#hand-the-warehouse-connection-to-a-teammate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your warehouse">
    Fill in the connection form for your warehouse and click **Test & save**. Lightdash creates a project with only a warehouse connection. No dbt project is imported and no dbt compilation runs. For the fields each warehouse needs, see [Connect a project](/integrations/connect-project).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your repo">
    If you already have models in a repository, click **Connect repo** so your team can ask questions with them. Otherwise click **Skip for now**; you can connect a repository later in project settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Connect [live data sources](/integrations/live-data-sources), such as PostHog under **Product analytics** and the Google connectors under **Marketing and search**. This step is optional, and it appears only when live data sources are available to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your team">
    Connect the integrations your team works in: Slack and Microsoft Teams under **Share answers**, and GitHub and GitLab under **Review changes**. This step is optional, and Lightdash skips it when the instance has none of these integrations configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    The **You're all set** page confirms your data is ready. Click **Ask your first question** to open a conversation with the agent, which starts by asking what's in your data and which questions are worth asking. Click **I'll look around first** to go to the project homepage instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start without a warehouse

On the **Connect your data** page, the live data source card lets you start from a tool instead of a warehouse. Depending on what your instance has configured, it offers PostHog alone, or PostHog, Google Analytics 4, and Search Console.

Choosing it creates a project with no warehouse and no dbt project, then takes you to **Connect your tools**, where you must connect at least one tool to continue. You can add a warehouse to the project later.

Self-hosted instances need an Enterprise license for [live data sources](/integrations/live-data-sources).

## Hand the warehouse connection to a teammate

If you're setting up Lightdash but don't hold the warehouse credentials, click **Invite a teammate to connect it** on the **Connect your data** page. Lightdash emails your teammate a setup invite that explains what they're being asked to do.

- The invite gives your teammate the **admin** role, which they need to create the project.
- When password sign-in is allowed, your teammate opens the emailed link and clicks **Continue as** with their email address. This activates their account without a password and takes them to the warehouse connection.
- Other instances show the standard invite form.

## The default agent

When the first project in an organization is created, Lightdash creates a default agent for it, named **Analytics agent**.

- The agent has [data access](/agents/data-access) and content tools turned on.
- User context is turned on, so the agent can address you by name.
- Lightdash creates the agent only when AI agents are available to the organization. Without them, the project is still created and the homepage shows a greeting with quick actions instead of the ask box.
- Preview projects don't get a default agent. A project that already has an agent doesn't get a second one.
- If creating the agent fails, the project is still created. You can add an agent yourself from [Set up agents](/agents/set-up-agents).

The agent's instructions end with "If no explores are available yet, use your warehouse tools to discover the schema and answer with SQL." That fallback applies only in [SQL mode](/agents/use-ai-agents#sql-mode), for people who can use the SQL Runner.

## The homepage on day one

After onboarding you land on the project homepage. For a new organization it opens with an ask box, a **Your data** block, and a **Getting started** panel that you can minimize.

On a project with no explores, the suggested questions above the ask box are based on your real warehouse tables, and each one can be answered with a SQL result.

### Getting started panel

The **Getting started** panel tracks what's left to set up. It appears during an organization's first 14 days, for people who can manage the project. It lists:

- **Connect your warehouse**
- **Define metrics and dimensions**
- **Build your first chart**
- **Try a chart type**
- **Apply your brand**
- **Invite your team**
- **Connect your Git repository**
- **Connect Slack**
- **Add more data sources**

Click **Skip for now** on an item to set it aside, and **Add back** to restore it.

## After onboarding

The project behaves like any other Lightdash project:

- Add more agents or customize the default one from [Set up agents](/agents/set-up-agents).
- Build your semantic layer by following the [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).
- Invite the rest of your team; see [Invite your team](/workspace-admin/invite-new-users).
