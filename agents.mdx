Lightdash agents let anyone on your team take a question as far as it goes. Ask, follow the next lead, and turn what you find into dashboards and data apps the rest of the team can pick up. Your semantic layer and the business context you maintain give everyone the same starting point, so people can explore on their own and still build on shared definitions.

## Ask more questions

[Set up an agent](/agents/set-up-agents) for your team, then ask about your metrics the way you'd ask a colleague. Compare segments and time periods, question the charts and tables that come back, and keep following up when an answer raises something new. Good analysis rarely stops at the first chart.

When you find something worth sharing, keep going in the same thread. Ask the agent to turn it into [charts and dashboards](/agents/enable-content-tools) or a [data app](/data-apps) your team can open and explore, using the tools you've enabled for that agent. For a question that needs more digging, use [deep research](/agents/deep-research) to work through it and get back a report.

## Get trustworthy answers

Build answer quality around [clear semantic definitions and focused instructions](/agents/effective-analytics-with-agents). Metric queries use your semantic layer's definitions and joins, with your project's permissions and user attributes. Inspect the query and visualization behind an answer to check its assumptions and results.

Maintain [project context](/agents/agent-context) for shared terminology and table-selection rules, [knowledge documents](/agents/knowledge-documents) for longer references, and [skills](/agents/custom-agent-skills) for repeatable analytical procedures. Keep each business rule in the appropriate place so agents can reuse it without contradictory guidance.

Curate [verified answers](/agents/verified-answers) as checked examples and run [evaluation suites](/agents/create-evaluation-suites) when you change instructions, context, or models. Review conversations through [visibility tools](/agents/visibility) and use the [Issues board](/agents/issues) to investigate findings and proposed fixes.

## Build your agentic data platform

As you expand to more teams and workflows, configure [data access](/agents/data-access) and [agent permissions](/agents/agent-permissions) for the work each agent should do. Manage [agents as code](/agents/agents-as-code) to version configuration, skills, and evaluation suites in Git and promote them between projects.

Use [AI writeback](/agents/ai-writeback) to propose changes to your data project through pull requests your team reviews. The [AI coding agent](/agents/ai-coding-agent) supports coding workflows in other connected repositories. For scheduled project maintenance, configure [Autopilot](/agents/autopilot) and review its recorded actions.

## Integrate everything

Bring agents into [Slack](/agents/set-up-slack-agents) or [embed them](/embed/embed-ai-agents) in your product. Turn an analysis into a [recurring delivery](/agents/use-ai-agents#schedule-a-recurring-delivery-from-a-conversation) when your team needs to follow it over time.

Connect external assistants to your analytics through the [Lightdash MCP server](/agents/lightdash-mcp), or give Lightdash agents tools from other services through [external MCP servers](/agents/connect-external-mcp-servers).
