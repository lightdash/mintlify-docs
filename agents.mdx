Explore your data, check the evidence behind an answer, and build reusable workflows for your team. Lightdash brings together your semantic layer, reusable business context, and tools for acting on an analysis.

## Ask more questions

Use [conversations](/agents/use-ai-agents) to investigate a change in a metric, compare segments or time periods, and follow up on what you find. An agent selects relevant models and fields, runs queries, and returns charts, tables, and explanations you can inspect.

For a longer investigation, [deep research](/agents/deep-research) works through a question and produces a report. When the data isn't modeled as an explore, people with SQL Runner access can use [SQL mode](/agents/use-ai-agents#sql-mode) for read-only warehouse queries, outside semantic-layer controls.

## Get trustworthy answers

Metric queries use the definitions and joins in your semantic layer, with your project's permissions and user attributes. [Data access controls](/agents/data-access) determine which explores and fields an agent can use and whether it can read query results. Inspect the query and visualization behind an answer to check its assumptions and results.

[Verified answers](/agents/verified-answers) give agents examples your team has checked. Use [evaluation suites](/agents/create-evaluation-suites) to test representative questions, [visibility tools](/agents/visibility) to review conversations and tool activity, and the [Issues board](/agents/issues) to review findings and proposed fixes.

## Build your agentic data platform

[Create specialized agents](/agents/set-up-agents) for your teams and business domains. Give them shared terminology through [project context](/agents/agent-context), longer references through [knowledge documents](/agents/knowledge-documents), and repeatable procedures through [custom skills](/agents/custom-agent-skills).

Enable tools to [create and edit saved content](/agents/enable-content-tools) or build [data apps](/data-apps) from an analysis. Use [AI writeback](/agents/ai-writeback) to propose changes to your data project through pull requests your team reviews. [Autopilot](/agents/autopilot) runs scheduled project maintenance separately from conversations, recording its actions for review.

## Integrate everything

Bring agents into [Slack](/agents/set-up-slack-agents) or [embed them](/embed/embed-ai-agents) in your product. Turn an analysis into a [recurring delivery](/agents/use-ai-agents#schedule-a-recurring-delivery-from-a-conversation) when your team needs to follow it over time.

Connect external assistants to your analytics through the [Lightdash MCP server](/agents/lightdash-mcp), or give Lightdash agents tools from other services through [external MCP servers](/agents/connect-external-mcp-servers). For work in other connected repositories, the [AI coding agent](/agents/ai-coding-agent) can prepare changes as pull requests.
